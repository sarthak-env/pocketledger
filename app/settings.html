About
💰 PocketLedger v1.0
A simple personal finance tracker. All data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage — nothing is ever sent to any server.

Note on currency: amounts are stored as-entered in your chosen currency. Changing the currency symbol updates how amounts display; it does not convert values.
⚠️

Reset All Data?

This permanently deletes all your expenses and resets settings to default. This cannot be undone.
